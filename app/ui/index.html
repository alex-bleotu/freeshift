        Activity

          Buttons light up when you press them on the Click. Click one to choose its action: gears, menu navigation, steering, view or workout controls.

          Swap shift up / down flips only the gear actions.

          FreeShift connects to a Zwift Click by itself. Don't pair it in Windows Bluetooth settings.

          A Click talks to one app at a time, so close BikeControl, Zwift or Zwift Companion while using FreeShift.

          The left pod of a Click v2 only works while Zwift has unlocked it in the last day; the right pod always works.

          Network: MyWhoosh finds FreeShift on your local network as an OpenBikeControl controller. In MyWhoosh open Connection, tap the OpenBikeControl icon (top right), pick FreeShift and confirm.

          On Windows, MyWhoosh needs Apple's Bonjour service; restart MyWhoosh if it was open before Bonjour started. If Windows asks about network access, allow private networks.

          Keyboard: FreeShift brings the window whose title starts with the text you set to the front and presses the key. MyWhoosh shifts with I and K. Keys: a letter or digit, Up, Down, Left, Right, Enter, Escape, Space, Tab, PageUp, PageDown, Home, End or F1 to F12. Leave a key empty to send nothing.

          For a Bluetooth device that only reports that something was pressed. One press is Tap, two quick presses are Double tap; set their actions on the controller above.

          Find the device's characteristic with freeshift-cli explore <name>.
